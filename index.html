02

Before / after

Starting from an AI-generated first pass and rewriting it into what actually shipped. Five different editorial instincts, side by side.

Please Take Care of Her
emotional tightening

Every chorus in the draft explained the feeling in an extra clause. The edit cuts the explanation and keeps the action — the same move, repeated on purpose, three times.

draftLove her gentle / Love her slower / Than I ever let myself learn to
finalLove her gentle / Love her slow
Hold her steady when she's shaking / When she's strong stand back and let her move
Hold her steady / Hold her tight
Move On
structural rearrangement

The hook was front-loaded and had nowhere to go. Moved it to the end of the chorus, then built an entirely new instrumental-break chant that didn't exist in the draft.

draftTime for you to move on, yeah / My dance floor's open, no more fear...
finalMy dance floor's open, no more fear... / Time for you to move on
(no equivalent section)
Move on / Move on / Move on / Time for you to move on
The Dating Game
comic timing & judgment

Tightened punchlines by cutting the explanation after the joke — and generalized one verse's nationality-specific joke into something universal, without losing the laugh.

draftwanna fly to India, get married, just me and you?
finalwanna fly to my country, get married, just me and you?
Sir, we just met. Do you even know my last name?
Sir, we just met.
What Disappears
specificity & embodiment

Traded vague, safe phrasing for concrete, physical language throughout — edited by ear, out loud, line by line.

draftStanding there, in socks
finalStanding there, cold feet
I know my worth... I know what I won't eat
I know my price... I know what I won't swallow
Miles and Magnets
personal specificity

Swapped generic imagery for details specifically true to a life in Seoul — down to how the front door actually locks.

draftThe quiet in my bedroom / Like money well spent
finalThe quiet in my bedroom / After all, I paid rent
I'll keep my front door key
I'll keep my front door code

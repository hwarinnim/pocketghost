Next to Me
jazz pop · swing · alt R&B
Listen ↗

A plain, unguarded love song — no invented character, no genre bit, just true. The device: the chorus repeats near verbatim, but one word changes each time — eyes → hands → lips — tracking physical intimacy deepening without ever stating it outright.

The Story of a Knight
medieval folk ballad
Listen ↗

Written in about thirty minutes — a narrative ballad about a knight who gives up his one chance at glory to save strangers. A frame device bookends the story: it opens "let me tell you about a knight" and closes "let me tell you about a king," the twist held back until the final line.

Verse 1 Gather round, folks, gather round And let me tell you about a knight He who lost his life in battle but Whose soul still sought great glory He rose up from his grave and Walked west a hundred miles Where the scarlet mountain stood Filled with ghosts from every land Verse 2 The one who killed the most, they said, And climbed up to the top Would become the demon king And rule the underworld The brave knight moved with speed Up the mountain step by step Leaving ghosts and demons dead In a trail under his feet Verse 3 But near the top the knight encountered A pure crowd of living souls Who were stranded in a cave And were shivering with fear Men and women, children and the old The knight knew that their end was nigh But the heart that had stopped beating Never once had lost its warmth Verse 4 He sheathed his sword aside And reached towards his face And in one swift movement He removed his own left eye "Here's my offering," said the knight To the fire-breathing mountain "Let these good people go, And let me rule your evil world." Verse 5 The mountain shook and trembled And the top began to open To free the innocent And for the Demon King to rise Final verse So, gather round, folks, gather round And let me tell you about a king He who saved the lives of many And lays ghosts and demons to waste.
